Privacy Policy — Lumo Lens

The developer prepared this draft based on how the app works. It has not been reviewed by legal counsel and should be reviewed before release.

Updated: November 19, 2026. This policy describes the data processed when you use Lumo Lens to translate text on your screen.

1. Account information

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google identifier, email address, name, and profile picture to create and authenticate your account. The server stores this information in MongoDB along with your role, account status, and plan. The app does not receive your Google password.

To prevent quota resets through account deletion and recreation, the server stores a separate record with a pseudonymous identifier, usage count, date, and expiry time. This record does not contain your email address, name, or original Google identifier. The count expires at midnight Vietnam time at the start of the next day. MongoDB automatically removes the record after it expires, even if your profile has already been deleted.

If you try the app without signing in, it generates a random identifier for the installation. The server stores a pseudonymous version of that identifier and the total usage count to enforce the trial limit across sessions. The trial record does not contain an email address or Google identifier, is not linked to an account profile, and does not reset daily.

The sign-in token is stored in the device's secure storage (SecureStore) and is valid for 30 days. App settings are stored on the device. Account information is retained until the account is deleted.

2. Recognized text and third-party AI providers

Text recognized on your screen is sent to the server for translation. When a model request is needed, the server forwards the text and any necessary context to the configured third-party AI model provider. The provider's own policy also applies to its processing. Avoid translating anything that contains passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data.

3. Screenshots are processed only on your device

The app processes screenshots on your device to recognize text (OCR). It does not send screenshots to the server or the AI provider. Only the text that needs translation is sent.

4. Device permissions

Screen capture permission lets the app capture the content you want to translate. Permission to display over other apps lets it show the floating translation button and translated text. You can stop translating and revoke these permissions in system settings. The corresponding features will then stop working.

5. Translation storage

The server caches translations by content in a shared cache that is not linked to accounts. MongoDB's time-to-live (TTL) setting expires cached entries after 30 days, although actual removal may occur later. Deleting your account does not delete entries from this shared cache.

6. Your rights

You can view your account information in the app, sign out to remove the token from your device, or permanently delete your account. If you sign in again after deletion, the app creates a new account.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, or account deletion requests, email phucnguyentrong1804@gmail.com. We process account deletion requests sent by email within 3 days of receipt, after verifying account ownership. See the account deletion instructions for how to submit a request.
