1. The service

Lumo Lens recognizes text on your device screen. Basic translation uses Google ML Kit on the device; optional AI translation uses a server. You choose whether to allow screen capture, enable the overlay, and select content to translate.

2. Accounts and usage limits

You can try basic translation without signing in, but you need an internet connection to authorize each new Guest translation. The trial limit applies per installation and does not reset daily.

Sign in with Google to use features that require an account. You are responsible for securing your device and sign-in session. The app displays your usage limits. Free basic translation uses the same daily quota online and offline. Offline turns reserved while connected are deducted from that quota and expire at midnight Vietnam time. Pro includes unlimited basic translation while your entitlement is valid. AI models and advanced features may require Pro or coins and are subject to their own limits. The daily quota resets at midnight Vietnam time. Deleting and recreating your profile with the same Google account does not reset it. The service may be interrupted if you lose your internet connection, reach your usage limit, or the provider is unavailable.
